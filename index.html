What Chronicle does

Everything a life journal should be

Built for real memories, Not just today's, but the ones from fifty years ago too.

◉

Life in decades

Browse your entire life at a glance. Zoom from decade to year to individual memory with a single click. See patterns, gaps, and concentrations across your whole timeline.

✦

Fuzzy dates

Not every memory has a precise date. Record memories as "Early 1985", "1985", or just "sometime in the 1980s". Mark any date as Uncertain to distinguish family history guesses from verified facts. Chronicle handles uncertainty gracefully.

👤

People & places

Tag the people and organisations in each memory. Build an index of everyone who appears in your life story. Filter and search across every memory they're part of.

✉

Share with the people there

Send a memory to someone who was part of it via a private link. They save it to their own Chronicle with one click. You both keep a private copy. Anyone can receive shared memories for free — sending requires a subscription.

🗺

Map view

See your memories plotted on a world map. Zoom into cities, countries, and continents. A visual record of everywhere your life has taken you.

🖨

Print your life story

Generate a print-ready document of your Chronicle or export as PDF to keep, share, or give to family.

📷

Photo import

Drop in photos and Chronicle reads the EXIF date automatically, placing them on your timeline without any manual date entry.

📨

Email import

Turn old emails into memories. Drop in a .eml file and Chronicle extracts the date, sender, and message to save as part of your story.

Why we built this
The stories we carry deserve more than a folder of photos and a fading memory.

Remembory exists because the online tools we use to record our lives are built for sharing in the moment, not for preserving across a lifetime. They're owned by companies, dependent on subscriptions, and designed to keep you engaged rather than to help you remember.

We think that's backwards. Your life story belongs to you. It should be private by default, portable, and beautiful.

Privacy isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

Chronicle runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. No analytics, no tracking, no server. Your memories are just a file on your device.
